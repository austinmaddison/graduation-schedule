# Thai Style Guide — DataGlass Mechanical Writing Rules

The voice, tone, and vocabulary skills tell you *what* to write. This file tells you *how to write it correctly in Thai script* — the mechanical and orthographic decisions that separate native Thai SaaS copy from translated-feeling SaaS copy.

Every rule here was distilled from the 15 surveyed Thai e-commerce sites and the DataGlass canon. When in doubt, defer to the canon (`thai-ecommerce-translation/reference/dataglass-marketing-canon.md`); this file fills the gaps the canon doesn't cover.

---

## 1. Spacing — the most-violated rule

Thai script traditionally uses **no spaces between words**. Modern Thai SaaS does. This is the most common mistake in machine-translated copy.

### Rules

**1.1 Between a Thai word and an English word: ALWAYS space.**
✅ DataGlass เปลี่ยนข้อมูลร้านค้าให้เป็น Actions
❌ DataGlassเปลี่ยนข้อมูลร้านค้าให้เป็นActions

**1.2 Between sentence-level thought units within Thai prose: use a space (Thai does not use a period to end body sentences).**
✅ "เพิ่มกำไรให้ร้าน ลดค่าโฆษณาที่เสียเปล่า ปรับ Listing ได้แม่นยำขึ้น"
❌ "เพิ่มกำไรให้ร้าน. ลดค่าโฆษณาที่เสียเปล่า. ปรับ Listing ได้แม่นยำขึ้น."

**1.3 Between commas and the next word: yes (Western style).**
✅ "Shopee, Lazada และ TikTok Shop"

**1.4 Around the em-dash —: yes, with a space on each side.**
✅ "Decision Engine — ระบบช่วยตัดสินใจ"
❌ "Decision Engine—ระบบช่วยตัดสินใจ"

**1.5 Around the slash / (when used for alternatives): yes, space on each side.**
✅ "ผู้ขาย / ร้านค้า / ธุรกิจ"
❌ "ผู้ขาย/ร้านค้า/ธุรกิจ"

**1.6 Around currency symbols: no space between ฿ and the amount.**
✅ ฿699 / ฿2,000 / ฿10,000
❌ ฿ 699 / 699฿

**1.7 Between number and Thai unit word: yes, space.**
✅ 1 เดือน, 30 วัน, 50 ออเดอร์, 24 ชั่วโมง
❌ 1เดือน, 30วัน

**1.8 Between number and percent: no space.**
✅ 30%, 40%
❌ 30 %, 30 เปอร์เซ็นต์

**1.9 Mega-sale dates (9.9, 11.11, 12.12): no space, period as separator.**
✅ 9.9, 11.11, 12.12, Pay Day
❌ 9 . 9, 9-9, 9/9

**1.10 Around the middle-dot · (for stat-strip separators): single space on each side.**
✅ Plans · 03  |  Status · เบต้า  |  Free trial · 1 month
❌ Plans·03

**1.11 "เร็ว ๆ นี้" (coming soon) — note the spaces.**
✅ "เร็ว ๆ นี้" (with thin spaces around the ๆ)
❌ "เร็วๆนี้" (no spaces — looks wrong)
Note: ๆ is the Thai repetition mark; modern style sets spaces around it in display copy.

## 2. Punctuation

### 2.1 Periods (full stops)

Thai prose **does not end sentences with periods.** Use spaces between sentence-units instead. Periods are reserved for:
- After numerals in numbered lists (1. 2. 3.)
- In English abbreviations inside Thai (24 ชม., ตร.ม., e.g.)
- In mega-sale date format (9.9, 11.11)
- After URL paths and decimal numbers

✅ "หยุดเดา เพิ่มกำไรด้วย Decision Engine สำหรับ Shopee, Lazada และ TikTok Shop"
❌ "หยุดเดา. เพิ่มกำไรด้วย Decision Engine สำหรับ Shopee, Lazada และ TikTok Shop."

**Exception:** in formal English-Thai bilingual contexts (legal pages, English-led copy with Thai gloss), periods may end English sentences inside the same paragraph. Keep the Thai prose period-free.

### 2.2 Commas

Used for:
- Separating items in a list (especially when items contain English): "Shopee, Lazada และ TikTok Shop"
- Separating thousands in numbers: 10,000 / 1,500,000
- Inside English clauses that retain their original punctuation

Avoid commas inside pure-Thai prose where a space would suffice.

### 2.3 Em-dash (—)

Heavy use; DataGlass's signature inline punctuation. Use for:
- Defining technical terms: "Decision Engine — ระบบช่วยตัดสินใจที่บอกว่าควรทำอะไรต่อ"
- Inline asides: "Mathematical Optimization — คณิตศาสตร์ที่เลือก Action ที่ดีที่สุด — ทำให้ DataGlass ต่างจาก dashboard"
- Connecting clauses for rhythm: "Connect once — Decide daily — Deploy in one click"

**Always** the em-dash (—), never the hyphen (-) or en-dash (–) for this purpose.

Mac shortcut: ⌥+Shift+− / Windows: Alt+0151 / HTML: `&mdash;`

### 2.4 Hyphen (-)

Used inside compound Thai-English action formulas:
✅ "เก็บ-แพ็ก-ส่ง" (the fulfillment 3-verb)

Also for breaking English compound words across lines (rare in Thai layout).

### 2.5 Question mark (?)

Yes, with **no space before**:
✅ "DataGlass ฟรีจริงเหรอ?"
❌ "DataGlass ฟรีจริงเหรอ ?"

Used in FAQ headings, hero questions, body copy that legitimately asks something.

### 2.6 Exclamation mark (!)

Maximum **one per page**, **no space before**:
✅ "เรามาโตไปด้วยกัน!"
❌ "เรามาโตไปด้วยกัน!!" / "เรามาโตไปด้วยกัน !"

If a sentence isn't earning the !, don't use it. DataGlass body copy is declarative.

### 2.7 Ellipsis

Use three plain ASCII periods: `...`
Never the Unicode ellipsis character `…` (renders inconsistently across Thai fonts).

✅ "เริ่มต้นที่..."
❌ "เริ่มต้นที่…"

### 2.8 Quotation marks

For inline quotes inside Thai prose: use standard `"..."` (straight or curly, consistent within a page).
For testimonials: opening `"` + Thai quote + closing `"`, followed by em-dash + name.

✅ "ตั้งแต่ใช้ DataGlass ตัดงบโฆษณาที่เสียเปล่าออกได้ ฿15,000/เดือน" — คุณ[Nickname] เจ้าของร้าน [Brand]

Don't use Thai-specific quote marks « » or 「 」.

### 2.9 Colon (:)

Used for:
- Defining sections inline ("Free trial: 1 month")
- After labels in stat strips and headers

Pattern: label + ":" + space + value. Inside bilingual contexts, you can also use the middle-dot · instead (see 1.10).

### 2.10 Parentheses ( )

Use for short asides and English-Thai bridges:
✅ "Bayesian inference (การเรียนรู้จากข้อมูลที่อัปเดตเรื่อย ๆ)"

Always paired. No space inside the parentheses, but spaces outside.

## 3. Numerals

### 3.1 Always Arabic numerals

✅ 1, 2, 3, 100, 1,000, 9.9, 11.11
❌ ๑, ๒, ๓, ๑๐๐, ๑,๐๐๐

Thai numerals (๑–๙) are reserved for ceremonial/legal contexts. Never in marketing copy.

### 3.2 Thousands separator

Western comma:
✅ 10,000 / 50,000 / 1,500,000
❌ 10.000 / 1'000

### 3.3 Decimals

Period as decimal point:
✅ 2.5x / 3.14 / ฿4.8k

### 3.4 Currency

`฿` symbol before the number, no space:
✅ ฿699 / ฿2,000 / ฿10,000 / ฿4.8k/วัน

In rare cases where the symbol can't render, use `บ.` or `THB`:
✅ "699 บ./เดือน" / "699 THB"

**Don't** use the word บาท in marketing copy when ฿ will do — ฿ is shorter and recognizable.

### 3.5 Numeric abbreviations

- `k` for thousand: ฿4.8k = 4,800 baht
- `M` for million: ฿1.5M
- `B` for billion (rare): ฿1.2B

Use these for dashboard-like data displays. In prose, write out the number for clarity (฿4,800/วัน instead of ฿4.8k/วัน on a page hero).

### 3.6 Percentages

Number + `%`, no space:
✅ 30%, 40%, 99.5%
❌ 30 %, 30 เปอร์เซ็นต์

### 3.7 Time periods

Number + space + Thai unit:
✅ 1 เดือน, 30 วัน, 24 ชั่วโมง, 7 วัน
✅ 24 ชม. (abbreviated)

For ranges: en-dash with no spaces inside numbers, spaces outside if Thai unit follows.
✅ 09:00–18:00 / 1–3 วัน

### 3.8 Dates

- **Marketing copy:** Western calendar year (2026, not 2569). Format: `1 มกราคม 2026` or `Jan 1, 2026`.
- **Mega-sale events:** keep verbatim format `9.9`, `11.11`, `12.12`, `Pay Day`.
- **URLs / data:** ISO `2026-01-01`.
- **Legal:** Buddhist Era acceptable if context requires (พ.ศ. 2569).

## 4. Capitalization in mixed Thai-English copy

Thai script has no capitals. But the English fragments inside Thai copy need careful case decisions.

### 4.1 Brand names — keep exact brand case

✅ **DataGlass** (not Dataglass, not datagrass, not DATAGLASS)
✅ **Shopee, Lazada, TikTok Shop** (not Shoppee, not Tiktok)
✅ **LINE OA, LINE SHOPPING** (LINE always ALL-CAPS — it's the brand spelling)
✅ **depa** (lowercase — it's the brand's chosen form), **DEPA TH** (UPPER for the program)
✅ **PromptPay** (camelCase — brand spelling)

### 4.2 Trinity / category terms — Title Case

These are proper nouns of our category:
✅ **Decision Engine**
✅ **Mathematical Optimization**
✅ **Mathematical Modeling**
✅ **Risk-Aware Optimization**
✅ **Robust Optimization**
✅ **Operations Research**

In rare in-table use, **Decision engine** (sentence case) appears as the pricing-comparison row label. Otherwise: Title Case.

### 4.3 Method terms — typically Title Case

✅ Bayesian inference, Causal inference, Monte Carlo simulation, Price elasticity

Mixed convention: the discipline word (Bayesian, Causal, Monte Carlo) is capitalized; the descriptor (inference, simulation, elasticity) is lowercase. This matches academic convention.

### 4.4 Brand-coined product nouns

✅ **Actions** (capital A) — the product object / queue
✅ **Action** (capital A, singular) — one recommendation
✅ **Deploy** (capitalized when used as the brand verb: "Deploy ในคลิกเดียว")
✅ **North Stars** (the three pillars)
✅ **Workspace** (when used as the product noun; "workspace" lowercase in generic prose acceptable)

### 4.5 Industry-standard acronyms — UPPER

✅ ROAS, True ROAS, GMV, COGS, ACOS, SKU, ERP, OMS, WMS, CRM, API, AI, ML, KPI, CVaR-95, PDPA, B2B

### 4.6 Common nouns from English — lowercase

When an English noun is used generically inside a Thai sentence (not as a brand or category term):
✅ voucher, bid, campaign, bundle, deploy *(verb)*, keyword, listing, event

Exception: when the same word is used as a brand action verb in an Action label, capitalize: "Adjust Voucher" but "ปรับ voucher ของคุณ"

### 4.7 Plan names — Title Case

✅ Starter, Pro, Partner

### 4.8 In ALL-CAPS section headers (bilingual pattern)

When a section uses the English-ALL-CAPS + Thai sub-heading pattern:
✅ "MATHEMATICAL OPTIMIZATION — โมเดลที่เลือก Action ที่ดีที่สุด"
✅ "DECISION ENGINE — ระบบช่วยตัดสินใจของ DataGlass"

Use ALL-CAPS only for the trinity-level section labels. Not for body copy.

## 5. Sentence-ending particles

### 5.1 Allowed only in testimonials

- **ค่ะ / คะ** — feminine politeness particle (ค่ะ at sentence end with falling tone, คะ as question marker / sentence-end with rising tone)
- **ครับ** — masculine politeness particle

In testimonials: one particle per quote, naturally placed at the end of a clause.

✅ "ตั้งแต่ใช้ DataGlass ขายของง่ายขึ้นเยอะค่ะ"
✅ "ลดงบโฆษณาที่เสียเปล่าได้ครับ"

### 5.2 Never in body copy

❌ "DataGlass ช่วยคุณได้นะคะ"
❌ "ลองดูสิคะ"
❌ "ใช้ฟรีเลยครับ"

Body copy is declarative. No particles.

### 5.3 Other particles to avoid in body copy

- **นะ** (softening / persuasive) — too informal
- **เลย** (emphatic) — too casual
- **สิ** (encouraging) — too pushy
- **น่ะ** (explanatory) — too informal

Reserve all of these for testimonials and FAQ answers if needed.

### 5.4 The one warm beat per page rule

The pricing free-tier callout uses **"เรามาโตไปด้วยกัน!"** — that's the one place DataGlass uses a warm, exclamatory Thai phrase. **Do not** add a second warm beat on the same page. Other pages: no warm beats at all.

## 6. Connective words

These connect clauses. DataGlass prefers comma-and-space over conjunction chains.

| English | Thai | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| and | **และ** | Use before the last item in a list. For lists separated by commas, "และ" before the final item. |
| or | **หรือ** | |
| but | **แต่** | |
| because | **เพราะ** / **เนื่องจาก** (formal) | |
| so that | **เพื่อ** / **เพื่อที่จะ** (formal) | |
| if | **ถ้า** / **หาก** (formal) | |
| while | **ขณะที่** | |
| instead of | **แทนที่จะ** / **แทน** | |

**DataGlass preference:** short connectives. **แต่** beats **แต่อย่างไรก็ตาม**. **เพื่อ** beats **เพื่อที่จะ**.

## 6b. ทับศัพท์ — when to transliterate for modern feel

Thai script has a long tradition of **ทับศัพท์** — transliterating English technical terms into Thai script (โมเดล, ดีพลอย, ฟีเจอร์, แพลตฟอร์ม, อัลกอริทึม). For DataGlass, this is the **modern middle register** between raw English and pure-Thai translation.

### When ทับศัพท์ is the right choice

- **Body prose connective tissue** between brand terms: "DataGlass ใช้ โมเดล Mathematical Optimization เพื่อ..."
- **Modern technical vocabulary** that has no clean Thai equivalent: อัลกอริทึม, แดชบอร์ด, ฟีเจอร์, แพลตฟอร์ม, อัปเดต, ดาวน์โหลด
- **UI labels** that need to feel native-Thai-tech: ฟีเจอร์, ดาวน์โหลด, อัปเดต
- **Casual verb forms** of brand actions: ดีพลอย (casual) vs Deploy (brand verb capitalized)

### When ทับศัพท์ is wrong

- **Brand category terms**: don't write ดีซีชั่นเอนจิน — keep "Decision Engine."
- **Long compound technical names**: don't write แมเทมาทิคัล ออปไทไมเซชัน — keep "Mathematical Optimization."
- **Academic method names**: don't write เบย์เซียน อินเฟอเรนซ์ — keep "Bayesian inference."
- **Pure Thai is more emotional**: prefer ปรับ over ออปไทไมซ์ when the verb of the win lands harder in Thai (ปรับโฆษณา > ออปไทไมซ์โฆษณา).

### The three-register palette

For any English concept, DataGlass has three valid forms:

| Register | Example for "model" | When |
|---|---|---|
| **1. English** | Mathematical Model / Mathematical Modeling | Brand spine, deck, hero proof line |
| **2. ทับศัพท์** | โมเดล | Body prose, connective tissue, casual references |
| **3. Pure Thai** | (none — would be awkward) | Reserve for the verbs of the win, not technical nouns |

A single sentence often uses all three. The writer's job is to pick the register that fits the sentence rhythm — not to force one register across the page.

See `strategic-vocabulary.md` Tier 9 for the full transliteration palette.

## 9. Word breaks and line wrapping

Thai script has no word boundaries — line breaks happen via word-segmentation algorithms. This affects how copy renders in narrow columns and mobile views.

### 9.1 In HTML/CSS

For Thai-heavy blocks, use:

```css
.thai-text {
  word-break: keep-all;
  line-break: strict;
  /* prevents English brand names from breaking mid-word */
  overflow-wrap: break-word;
}
```

### 9.2 No-break for branded pairs

If a brand pair must not break across lines (e.g. "DataGlass Labs", "TikTok Shop", "Pay Day"), use a non-breaking space:

```html
DataGlass&nbsp;Labs
TikTok&nbsp;Shop
Pay&nbsp;Day
```

Or in Markdown / plain text where HTML isn't an option, simply test the rendered output in narrow viewports.

### 9.3 At Thai/English boundaries

Always include a space when an English word is adjacent to Thai. This gives the renderer a clean break point and reads naturally.

## 10. Bold and italic

### 10.1 Bold

Use bold for:
- Key terms (`**Decision Engine**`, `**Actions**`, `**Deploy**`)
- Section headers (in Markdown) and emphasis points
- The trinity / methods on first mention in a section
- Numbers and metrics when they're the point of the sentence ("**฿8,400/เดือน**")

Thai bold renders well in modern web fonts. Use freely but not promiscuously.

### 10.2 Italic

Thai fonts often lack a true italic — they render as oblique or revert to regular. Use italic sparingly:
- For inline thought-asides: *("the math behind it")*
- For book/research titles
- For English technical terms when emphasis is needed (preserves convention from English typography)

Avoid italic for entire Thai sentences — they may render poorly.

### 10.3 Underline

Reserve for hyperlinks. Don't underline for emphasis.

### 10.4 ALL-CAPS

Used for English section labels in the bilingual header pattern (see §4.8). Not for Thai script (impossible) and not for body emphasis.

## 11. Lists

### 11.1 Numbered lists

`1. 2. 3.` — Arabic numerals + period + space. Continue numbering across the list.

```
1. ปรับโฆษณา
2. ปรับ Listing
3. เข้าร่วม Campaign
```

### 11.2 Bulleted lists

In Markdown: `-` (preferred), or `*`.
In rendered display: `•` (the standard bullet).

Don't end Thai bullet items with periods.

### 11.3 Definition lists (term : description)

Use em-dash:
```
Decision Engine — ระบบช่วยตัดสินใจที่บอกว่าควรทำอะไรต่อ
```

### 11.4 Stat strips (pipe-separated or middle-dot-separated)

```
Plans · 03  |  Status · เบต้า  |  Free trial · 1 month  |  Lock-in · ไม่มี
```

Use middle-dot (·) between label and value, single space on each side. Pipe (|) between items, with at least one space on each side. The pricing page uses this verbatim.

## 12. URLs, paths, and code

### 12.1 URLs in body

Keep URLs in their natural form, not translated:
✅ `dataglasslabs.com`
✅ `/sign-up`, `/pricing`, `#how`

Never translate URL paths into Thai script.

### 12.2 Inline code formatting

For technical terms in Markdown, use backticks: `Mathematical Optimization`, `Action`, `Deploy`. Especially useful in research / technical content.

### 12.3 File paths

Use forward slashes, English-only:
✅ `dg-marketing/layers/product/app/data/marketing-home.ts`
❌ Never localize.

## 13. Quotation conventions for testimonials

The DataGlass testimonial pattern is:

```
"[Quote in Thai — usually starts with ตั้งแต่ใช้ DataGlass...]" — คุณ[Nickname] เจ้าของร้าน [Brand]
```

Rules:
- Standard double quotes (curly or straight, consistent within page).
- Em-dash between quote and attribution.
- "คุณ" prefix before the nickname (not a full name; not a title).
- "เจ้าของร้าน" or "เจ้าของแบรนด์" — never "ผู้ก่อตั้ง" (too formal) or "เจ้าของกิจการ" (PEAK register).
- Brand name in its original form (Shopee shop name, brand spelling preserved).
- Particle ค่ะ / ครับ optional inside the quote.

Example:
> "ตั้งแต่ใช้ DataGlass ตัดงบโฆษณาที่เสียเปล่าออกได้ ฿15,000/เดือน คุ้มมากค่ะ" — **คุณยุ้ย เจ้าของร้าน OVARAN**

## 14. Common pitfalls (read this before shipping)

The mechanical mistakes DataGlass copy must avoid:

| Pitfall | Wrong | Right |
|---|---|---|

## 15. Quick mechanical-pass checklist

Before any Thai DataGlass surface ships:

- [ ] Every Thai/English boundary has a space.
- [ ] No periods end Thai sentences in body copy.
- [ ] All numerals are Arabic (1, 2, 3 — never ๑, ๒, ๓).
- [ ] Currency: ฿ + amount, no space.
- [ ] Number + Thai unit: with space (1 เดือน, 50 ออเดอร์).
- [ ] Percentages: number + %, no space (30%).
- [ ] Mega-sale dates verbatim: 9.9 / 11.11 / 12.12 / Pay Day.
- [ ] Em-dashes — with spaces on both sides — for definitions and asides.
- [ ] Maximum one ! per page; no space before it.
- [ ] No particles (ค่ะ/นะ/เลย/สิ) in body copy. Testimonials only.
- [ ] Brand names in original case: DataGlass, Shopee, Lazada, TikTok Shop, LINE, depa, DEPA TH.
- [ ] Trinity terms Title Case: Decision Engine, Mathematical Optimization, Mathematical Modeling, Risk-Aware Optimization.
- [ ] Acronyms UPPER: ROAS, GMV, SKU, COGS, ACOS, etc.
- [ ] Actions capitalized as product noun; Deploy capitalized as brand verb.
- [ ] One stock spelling per page (recommend: สต็อก).
- [ ] No ฯลฯ / อาทิเช่น / ดำเนินการ / ผู้ประกอบการ.
- [ ] Plain ASCII ellipsis `...`, not `…`.
- [ ] No-break spaces around branded pairs ("Pay Day", "TikTok Shop") in narrow-column layouts.

## 16. When to break the rules

This style guide is the default. Break a rule when:

1. The DataGlass marketing canon already breaks it. (Canon wins.)
2. A legal page requires period-ended Thai sentences for clarity. (Legal pages have their own conventions.)
3. A platform constraint forces a specific format (e.g., search-engine result snippets limit punctuation).
4. A testimonial's authentic voice includes particles or exclamations — preserve the customer's voice.

In all other cases: ship the rules.
